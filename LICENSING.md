# Licensing status

**Public release is blocked until the repository's rights holders approve a
repository-wide license covering the benchmark implementation.**

No repository-wide license grant was present in the supplied benchmark source.
This applies to reused benchmark models, adapters, observables and orchestration;
this notice grants no redistribution or reuse rights. The authors must confirm
ownership and any incorporated third-party material, approve an appropriate
license, and supply the corresponding copyright/license notices before public
distribution. The NanoKMC solver's BSD license does not automatically license
this separate benchmark repository.

There is intentionally no file named `LICENSE` until a valid license grant is
established. Citation metadata and source provenance are not substitutes for one.

External solver sources are downloaded separately into ignored `sources/`; their
licenses remain in those exports. They are not vendored or relicensed here.
[Third-party attribution](docs/THIRD_PARTY.md) identifies those notices. Any later
distribution of upstream sources, generated solver code or linked binaries needs
its own review against the relevant upstream license terms.
